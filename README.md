# SwordFix (Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge)

---

## 📌 Overview

**SwordFix** is a lightweight, client-side mod for Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge that removes the first-person weapon and tool lowering animation during attack cooldown.

In vanilla Minecraft 1.20.1:
1. When you attack, the cooldown drops to 0, which causes the first-person hand renderer (`ItemInHandRenderer`) to dip the weapon down off-screen.
2. Weapon durability updates upon hitting targets cause an annoying re-equip twitch.

**SwordFix** keeps weapons and tools raised in front of you at all times while keeping damage calculations, attack speed, and vanilla mechanics 100% intact.

---

## ✨ Features

- **No Weapon Dipping:** Swords, axes, pickaxes, and modded weapons stay in front of you after attacking.
- **Vanilla Combat Mechanics:** Damage scaling, attack cooldown timings, and crosshair cooldown indicators remain completely vanilla.
- **Normal Item Switching:** Smooth equip animations still play when switching between hotbar slots.
- **Universal Mod Support:** Fully compatible with other Forge 1.20.1 mods and shaders.
- **Client-Side Only:** No server installation required.

---

## 🚀 Installation

1. Download `SwordFix-1.20.1-1.0.0.jar`.
2. Place the jar into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
3. Launch Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge.
